Connecting Businesses. Creating Opportunities.

Bharat Vyapaar is a business networking platform built to help businesses connect, collaborate and explore growth opportunities beyond their home territories. Our aim is to make business expansion more accessible, particularly for smaller organisations with limited infrastructure and resources.

Whether you are looking to expand your business, discover new partners, build supplier relationships or connect with businesses across industries and geographies, Bharat Vyapaar aims to create meaningful opportunities through direct business connections.

OUR FOUNDER

Amit Agarwal

Bharat Vyapaar was founded by Amit Agarwal, Founder & CEO of Franchise Horizon, in 2026. With over a decade of experience in franchise and business consultancy and 30 years of overall experience across various business domains, Amit brings a broad perspective on business growth and expansion.

A PGDBA from ICFAI, he has supported clients in exploring opportunities across India and international markets, including Asia-Pacific, the Middle East, Africa and Europe. He is supported by a team of professionals and associates offering expertise in research, legal, strategy, marketing, promotion and physical fulfilment.

OUR PURPOSE

Why Bharat Vyapaar?

Bharat Vyapaar was created with a clear purpose: to help businesses grow beyond their existing territories while keeping acquisition costs low. We aim to support smaller organisations that want to expand but may have limited resources or infrastructure.

Our approach focuses on enabling people and businesses to connect and collaborate directly, without joining fees, membership fees or brokerage.

OUR COMMUNITY

Our Network & Community

From an initial group of approximately 60 members, Bharat Vyapaar has grown to a network of over 1,200 people representing different business domains, industries and geographical locations, as stated in our current organisational profile.

We aim to strengthen these connections and create opportunities for meaningful business interactions, collaboration and growth.

OUR VISION

Our Team & Vision

Bharat Vyapaar is supported by a team of 16 professionals committed to developing the platform and expanding its reach nationally and globally.

Our vision is to evolve from a virtual business networking platform towards a broader ecosystem that enables businesses to build stronger relationships, explore new markets and pursue sustainable growth.
